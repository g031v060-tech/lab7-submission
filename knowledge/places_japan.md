# Places in Japan (日本の観光地)

## Chūson-ji Temple (中尊寺) — Hiraizumi, Iwate (岩手県平泉町)
- Category: Buddhist temple / 仏教寺院; Hiraizumi UNESCO World Heritage area / 世界遺産「平泉」
- Highlights: Konjikidō Golden Hall (金色堂) retains its gold-covered interior; the Sankōzō museum displays Buddhist art and historical objects / 金色堂と讃衡蔵の仏教美術・文化財
- Access: JR Hiraizumi Station (平泉駅), then the local Rurunto loop bus or an Iwate-Kenpoku bus serving Chūson-ji; check the current timetable / 平泉駅から町内巡回バス「るんるん」等
- Cost: Standard adult admission to Konjikidō and Sankōzō is ¥1,000; parking is separate / 大人の通常拝観料1,000円、駐車場別
- Best season: Spring greenery (新緑) or autumn leaves (紅葉); seasonal preference is an estimate and foliage timing varies / 季節のおすすめは目安
- Source: https://chusonji.or.jp/language_en/ ; https://chusonji.or.jp/worship/access.html (checked 2026-11)

## Zuiganji Temple (瑞巌寺) — Matsushima, Miyagi (宮城県松島町)
- Category: Rinzai Zen temple / 臨済宗の禅寺; National Treasure buildings / 国宝建造物
- Highlights: The main hall and Kuri preserve Momoyama-period architecture; the temple is associated with Date Masamune / 本堂・庫裏と伊達政宗ゆかりの歴史
- Access: About 10 minutes on foot from JR Matsushima-Kaigan Station (松島海岸駅); about 25 minutes from JR Matsushima Station / 松島海岸駅から徒歩約10分
- Cost: Adult admission ¥1,000; the temple ticket includes the Seiryuden museum / 大人1,000円、青龍殿を含む
- Best season: Spring and autumn (春・秋) are pleasant seasons for walking the grounds; this is a seasonal suggestion, not a guarantee / 季節のおすすめは目安
- Source: https://www.zuiganji.or.jp/english/ (checked 2026-11)

## Nebuta Museum WA RASSE (ねぶたの家 ワ・ラッセ) — Aomori, Aomori (青森県青森市)
- Category: Festival museum / 祭りの博物館; Aomori Nebuta culture / 青森ねぶた
- Highlights: Full-size floats from the festival show their paper, wire and lighting craft; scheduled Haneto dance experiences introduce festival music and movement / 大型ねぶた山車と跳人の体験
- Access: About a 1-minute walk from Aomori Station (青森駅) / 青森駅から徒歩約1分
- Cost: Adult museum admission ¥620; school-age rates differ / 大人620円
- Best season: August festival season (ねぶた祭); the museum also displays floats outside the festival period, but check its annual closure notices / 祭り期間と休館日を事前確認
- Source: https://www.nebuta.jp/warasse/foreign/english.html (checked 2026-11)

## Kenrokuen Garden (兼六園) — Kanazawa, Ishikawa (石川県金沢市)
- Category: Japanese landscape garden / 日本庭園; adjacent to Kanazawa Castle Park / 金沢城公園
- Highlights: Kotoji lantern (徽軫灯籠), ponds, bridges and planted slopes form a walk-through garden whose appearance changes with the seasons / 徽軫灯籠・池・橋と四季の景色
- Access: Take a bus from Kanazawa Station (金沢駅) toward Kenroku-en-shita or Hirosaka, then walk to a garden gate; the prefecture lists several routes / 金沢駅から路線バス、各停留所から徒歩
- Cost: Adult admission ¥320; ages 6–17 ¥100 / 大人320円、6〜17歳100円
- Best season: Cherry blossoms (桜), autumn colors (紅葉) or winter snow supports (雪吊り); exact displays vary by year / 見頃は年により変動
- Source: https://shiro-niwa.pref.ishikawa.lg.jp/kenrokuen-garden/e/info.html ; https://shiro-niwa.pref.ishikawa.lg.jp/kenrokuen-garden/e/access.html ; https://shiro-niwa.pref.ishikawa.lg.jp/kenrokuen-garden/e/season.html (checked 2026-11)

## Osaka Castle Museum (大阪城天守閣) — Osaka, Osaka (大阪府大阪市)
- Category: Castle museum / 城郭博物館; historic park / 史跡公園
- Highlights: Exhibitions explain Osaka Castle's changing history and display historical objects; the upper-floor observation area overlooks the park and city / 大阪城の歴史資料と展望台
- Access: Osaka Metro Tanimachi 4-chome or Morinomiya, JR Osaka-jō-koen or Morinomiya, and other nearby stations serve the park; follow park signs to the Main Tower / 地下鉄・JR各駅から大阪城公園を通って天守閣へ
- Cost: Adult admission ¥1,200; university and high-school students ¥600 with ID; children 15 and under free with ID / 大人1,200円、大学・高校生600円
- Best season: Spring cherry blossoms (桜) and autumn park colors (紅葉) are seasonal suggestions; dates and conditions vary / 季節のおすすめは目安
- Source: https://www.osakacastle.net/pdf/leaflet/english_202604.pdf ; https://www.osakacastle.net/access/ (checked 2026-11)

## Hiroshima National Peace Memorial Hall (国立広島原爆死没者追悼平和祈念館) — Hiroshima, Hiroshima (広島県広島市)
- Category: Peace memorial and archive / 平和記念施設・資料館
- Highlights: The hall preserves victims' names and photographs and shares survivor memoirs and messages for peace / 原爆死没者の情報と被爆体験記を通じた平和学習
- Access: From JR Hiroshima Station, take a bus or streetcar to Hon-dori or Genbaku Dome-mae, then walk about 5 minutes / 広島駅からバス・路面電車で本通または原爆ドーム前、徒歩約5分
- Cost: Admission is free / 入館無料
- Best season: Open throughout the year except December 30–31; a quiet indoor visit works in any season / 12月30・31日を除き通年
- Source: https://www.hiro-tsuitokinenkan.go.jp/en/visit/index.html (checked 2026-11)

## Itsukushima Shrine (嚴島神社) — Hatsukaichi, Hiroshima (広島県廿日市市)
- Category: Shinto shrine / 神社; UNESCO World Heritage Site / 世界文化遺産
- Highlights: The shrine's halls, corridors and great Ōtorii gate stand in a coastal setting where the view changes with the tide / 社殿・回廊と海上の大鳥居、潮位で変わる景観
- Access: JR Hiroshima Station to JR Miyajimaguchi Station is about 30 minutes, followed by a walk to the port and an approximately 10-minute ferry to Miyajima / 広島駅から宮島口駅、徒歩で港へ、フェリー約10分
- Cost: Adult shrine admission ¥300; Treasure Hall and combined tickets have separate prices / 大人拝観料300円、宝物館等は別料金
- Best season: Spring or autumn (春・秋) is a seasonal suggestion; check tide times because the shoreline view changes / 潮見表を確認
- Source: https://www.itsukushimajinja.jp/en-sp/admission.html ; https://www.itsukushimajinja.jp/en-sp/access.html (checked 2026-11)

## Tokyo National Museum (東京国立博物館) — Tokyo, Tokyo (東京都台東区)
- Category: National museum / 国立博物館; Japanese and Asian art and archaeology / 日本・アジアの美術と考古
- Highlights: Galleries cover Japanese art, Asian art, archaeology and Hōryū-ji treasures; objects rotate to protect collections / 日本美術・東洋美術・考古資料・法隆寺宝物
- Access: About a 10-minute walk from JR Ueno or Uguisudani Station; several Ueno-area subway stations are also within walking distance / JR上野駅・鶯谷駅から徒歩約10分
- Cost: TNM Collection Exhibition admission is ¥1,000 for adults and ¥500 for university students; special exhibitions may cost extra / 総合文化展は大人1,000円、大学生500円、特別展は別料金
- Best season: Year-round indoor visit; exhibition rooms and some galleries change, so check the current schedule / 屋内展示は通年、展示替えあり
- Source: https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_free_page/index.php?id=113l&lang=en ; https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_free_page/?id=156&lang=en (checked 2026-11)

## Nara National Museum (奈良国立博物館) — Nara, Nara (奈良県奈良市)
- Category: National museum / 国立博物館; Buddhist art / 仏教美術
- Highlights: The museum presents Buddhist sculpture and decorative art, including works connected with Nara's historic collections / 仏像・仏教美術・工芸
- Access: About a 15-minute walk from Kintetsu Nara Station (近鉄奈良駅) / 近鉄奈良駅から徒歩約15分
- Cost: Visit Nara lists adult admission at ¥700 and college students at ¥350; its page was last updated 2025-11, so treat these as estimates and recheck current exhibition prices / 掲載料金は2025-11更新のため目安、最新料金を再確認
- Best season: Year-round indoor exhibitions; hours, Monday closures and special exhibitions can change / 屋内展示は通年、休館日・特別展を確認
- Source: https://www.visitnara.jp/venues/A00430/ (checked 2026-11; page last updated 2025-11)

## Fushimi Inari Taisha Shrine (伏見稲荷大社) — Kyoto, Kyoto (京都府京都市伏見区)
- Category: Shinto shrine / 神社; head shrine of Inari worship / 稲荷信仰の総本宮
- Highlights: Thousands of vermilion torii gates line the mountain path on sacred Mount Inari / 稲荷山へ続く朱色の千本鳥居
- Access: Short walk from JR Inari Station; about 5 minutes on foot from Keihan Fushimi-inari Station / JR稲荷駅すぐ、京阪伏見稲荷駅から徒歩約5分
- Cost: Shrine grounds are free to visit / 境内無料
- Best season: Grounds are open year-round; season preference is an estimate, and crowds vary / 通年参拝可、混雑は時期により変動
- Source: https://www.the-kansai-guide.com/en/directory/item/11818/ (checked 2026-11; page last updated 2026-06)
